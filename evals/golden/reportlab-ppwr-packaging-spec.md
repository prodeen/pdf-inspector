# Packaging Technical Specification – PPWR Assessment Ready

## General Information

Company: Prodeen Foods

Packaging ID: PF-PET-500ML-STD

Application: Carbonated Soft Drink Bottle

Country of Manufacture: Italy

## Material Composition

Primary Material: Polyethylene Terephthalate (PET) – 70%

Recycled Content: 30% rPET (post-consumer)

Additives: Food-grade stabilizers, acetaldehyde scavengers

Closure: HDPE cap with PP liner

## Physical Characteristics

Height: 210 mm

Diameter: 65 mm

Weight: 24 g ± 0.5 g

Volume: 500 ml nominal

Pressure Resistance: Suitable for carbonated beverages up to 6 bar

## Design for Recycling

Clear PET body compatible with EU recycling streams

Label: BOPP with washable adhesive

Cap tethered (compliant with EU Directive 2019/904)
