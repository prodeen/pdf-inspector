# 2. Ingredient Overview & Regulatory Status

|ATTRIBUTE|DETAIL|
|---|---|
|Chemical identity|Arachidonic acid, C20:4 n-6 — long-chain polyunsaturated omega-6 fatty acid.|
|Production organism|*Mortierella alpina* (oleaginous filamentous fungus; not GMO; not pathogenic).|
|Process|Submerged fermentation → biomass harvesting → drying → solvent or non-solvent extraction → degumming → bleaching → deodorisation → standardisation to ≥40% ARA.|
|Primary application|Infant formula (40–60 mg per 100 kcal; typically blended with DHA at 1:1 or 2:1 ARA:DHA ratio).|
|Other uses|Adult nutritional supplements; clinical nutrition for premature infants; fortified dairy; sports nutrition.|
|FDA status|GRAS — Notices GRN 958 and GRN 1186 for fungal oil ≥40% ARA from *M. alpina*.|
|EU status|Authorised under Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2016/127 for infant/follow-on formula. Addition is **optional**, capped at 1% of total fat content if added. The post-crisis policy debate is active: regulators face a fork between (a) making ARA mandatory with stringent sourcing controls, or (b) revisiting its functional necessity in the regulated composition.|
|Global acceptance|Approved in 99+ countries for infant nutrition; Codex Alimentarius CXS 72-1981 framework.|

# 12. Action Plan

**Key Takeaways**

- Cereulide cannot be processed out of contaminated ARA oil — prevention at fermentation is the only control.
- Single-source dependency on Chinese fermentation is the primary supply-chain vulnerability.
- Fraud risk is elevated post-crisis.
- Legal exposure via French criminal investigations is an active, evolving risk; expected civil/consumer litigation is the larger downstream cost driver.
