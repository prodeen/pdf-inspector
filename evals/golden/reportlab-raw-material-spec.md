# Raw Material Specification

Code: 1072

Material Name: Roasted Hazelnuts

## 1. Description

Whole or chopped hazelnuts that have been dry-roasted to enhance flavor and aroma, used in confectionery and bakery products.

## 2. Composition

|Component|Percentage (%)|
|---|---|
|Hazelnuts|100|

## 3. Physical and Chemical Properties

|Parameter|Specification|
|---|---|
|Appearance|Uniformly roasted brown nuts|
|Odor|Characteristic nutty aroma|
|Taste|Rich roasted nut flavor|
|Moisture|≤2.0%|
|Oil Content|≥60%|
|Defective Nuts|≤3%|

## 4. Microbiological Standards

|Parameter|Limit|
|---|---|
|Total Plate Count|≤10,000 CFU/g|
|Yeasts & Molds|≤100 CFU/g|
|Coliforms|<10 CFU/g|
|Salmonella|Absent in 25 g|
|Aflatoxins (B1+B2+G1+G2)|<10 ppb|

## 5. Allergen Information

Contains tree nuts (hazelnuts). May contain traces of peanuts and other nuts.

## 6. Packaging

Packed in vacuum-sealed or nitrogen-flushed food-grade bags to maintain freshness.

## 7. Storage and Shelf Life

Store in a cool, dry place at 10–15°C. Shelf life: 12 months under recommended conditions.

## 8. Country of Origin

Produced from cultivated hazelnuts (Corylus avellana), origin depending on supplier (e.g., Turkey, Italy).
