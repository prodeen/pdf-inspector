# Whole Roasted Almonds (1073)

Product Data Sheet

Whole Roasted Almonds (Lightly Roasted)

Product Code: 1073

## 1. Product Identification

Trade Name: Whole Roasted Almonds (Lightly Roasted)

Product Code: 1073

Classification: Natural Food Ingredient / Tree Nut

CAS Number: Not applicable (natural agricultural product)

Description: Whole almonds that have been lightly roasted to enhance flavor and aroma while maintaining structural integrity and nutritional properties.

## 2. Product Description

Whole Roasted Almonds (Lightly Roasted) are premium-grade almonds subjected to a controlled light roasting process. This preserves their natural sweetness, crunchy texture, and characteristic nutty profile.

## 3. Applications

- Snacks and nut mixes
- Confectionery, chocolates, and pralines
- Granolas and cereal bars
- Bakery fillings and toppings
- Dairy and plant-based yogurt inclusions
