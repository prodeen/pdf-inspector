Description: Natural flaky sea salt crystals obtained by solar evaporation and traditional harvesting methods.

## 2. Product Description

Sea Salt Flakes are delicate, pyramid-shaped salt crystals produced through natural solar evaporation of seawater. They are valued for their unique texture, clean flavor, and premium culinary appeal. Ideal for gourmet seasoning and finishing applications.

## 3. Applications

- Culinary finishing salt for meat, fish, salads, and vegetables
- Toppings for baked goods and desserts
- Premium snack seasoning
- Ingredient in gourmet spice blends and rubs
